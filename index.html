How to Get a Gemini API Key

1

Go to Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com) and sign in with your Google Account.

2

Click on "Create API key".

3

Copy your generated key (starts with AIzaSy...) and paste it into the key box on this page.

Privacy & Local Storage:

Your key is stored strictly inside your browser's local storage (localStorage) and is never sent anywhere except Google's official Gemini API endpoint.
